# UICP — Pricing

UICP pricing is determined by deployment scale, sector coverage,
enforcement volume, and the operational risk profile of the engagement.
All fees are negotiated directly with each client.

## Detection & Enforcement Services

| Service | Unit | Indicative Fee (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Payroll enforcement (ghost worker detection) | Per district / per run | Negotiated |
| Procurement compliance verification | Per entity / per run | Negotiated |
| Pension & gratuity auditing | Per entity / per run | Negotiated |
| Custom constraint enforcement | Per engagement | Negotiated |

## Ongoing Coverage

Quarterly, annual, and multi‑district coverage agreements are
available. Pricing reflects the number of enforcement runs,
the complexity of the constraint set, and the required support
level.

## Enterprise & Government

National‑scale deployments across multiple sectors and districts
are priced based on infrastructure requirements, dedicated
personnel, security provisions, and the number of enforcement
endpoints. Contact directly for a scoping engagement.

## Commercial & Fintech

Organisations deploying UICP for lending, insurance, healthcare,
or other commercial AI governance should contact directly for
pricing separate from the detection‑services schedule above.

---

All fees are invoiced in United States dollars. Payment terms
are net thirty days. Service Level Agreements are defined per
engagement.
