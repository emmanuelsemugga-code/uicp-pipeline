# UICP Pricing

**Effective June 2026 — Pilot Phase**

UICP is foundational infrastructure for AI governance. The pricing model
reflects this: a free pilot tier for early partners, a standard tier for
production deployments, and an enterprise tier for regulated institutions
requiring SOC 2 Type II certification and dedicated support.

All prices are in United States dollars. Payment terms are net 30 days.
Invoices are issued monthly.

---

## Tier 1 — Pilot (Free)

**For early partners validating UICP in a non‑production environment.**

**What's included:**
- Full access to the UICP enforcement engine (Phase 1‑5)
- REST API with API‑key authentication
- Up to 1,000 enforcement decisions per month
- One constraint set (up to 10 constraints)
- Community support via GitHub Issues
- Weekly audit bundle export

**Limitations:**
- Single tenant only
- No SLA
- No cryptographic key management support (operator manages keys)
- No SOC 2 Type II certification

**Duration:** 30 days, renewable by mutual agreement.

**Cost:** Free

**Who this is for:** Fintech lenders, healthcare providers, and government
agencies evaluating UICP for a specific use case.

---

## Tier 2 — Standard

**For organisations deploying UICP in production with a single use case.**

**What's included:**
- Everything in Pilot
- Up to 100,000 enforcement decisions per month
- Up to 5 constraint sets
- Multi‑tenant support (up to 3 tenants)
- Email support (response within 1 business day)
- Monthly audit bundle export with verification
- Encrypted personal data store with key management

**Add‑ons (priced separately):**
- Additional 100,000 decisions/month: $100/month
- Additional constraint set: $50/month
- Additional tenant: $75/month

**SLA:** 99.5% uptime during business hours (08:00‑18:00 EAT, Monday‑Friday).

**Cost:** $500/month

**Who this is for:** Fintech lenders and healthcare providers with a single
AI model in production that requires deterministic constraint enforcement.

---

## Tier 3 — Enterprise

**For regulated institutions requiring full compliance, dedicated support,
and custom integration.**

**What's included:**
- Everything in Standard
- Unlimited enforcement decisions
- Unlimited constraint sets
- Unlimited tenants
- SOC 2 Type II certification (included)
- Dedicated support engineer (Slack + email, response within 4 hours)
- Quarterly constraint review with UICP engineering team
- Custom integration support (up to 20 hours)
- Priority feature requests

**SLA:** 99.9% uptime, 24/7.

**Cost:** $2,000/month

**Who this is for:** Banks, insurance companies, government tax authorities,
and international organisations deploying UICP across multiple AI systems
and jurisdictions.

---

## Notes

- All prices are in United States dollars.
- Payment is due within 30 days of invoice.
- Late payments may result in service suspension after 15 days' notice.
- Clients may upgrade or downgrade at the end of any billing period.
- The Pilot tier is available to a maximum of 5 organisations at any time.
- Volume discounts are available for Enterprise deployments exceeding
  1,000,000 decisions per month.
