# UICP Payment Terms

**For Standard and Enterprise clients.**

---

## Accepted Payment Methods

- **Bank transfer** (preferred)
  - Bank account details provided on invoice
  - Payments in USD, EUR, or UGX
- **Mobile money** (Uganda only)
  - MTN Mobile Money
  - Airtel Money
- **International wire transfer**
  - SWIFT details provided on invoice
  - Client bears all intermediary bank charges

---

## Billing Cycle

- Invoices are issued on the **1st of each month** for the month ahead.
- Payment is due within **30 calendar days** of the invoice date.
- For new clients, the first invoice is issued on the service start date
  and is pro‑rated for the remaining days of that month.

---

## Late Payment Policy

- A reminder is sent **7 days** after the due date.
- A second reminder is sent **14 days** after the due date.
- If payment is not received within **30 days** of the due date, UICP
  reserves the right to:
  - Suspend API access until payment is received.
  - Apply a late fee of **1.5% per month** on the outstanding balance.
- Service is restored within **1 business day** of payment confirmation.

---

## Refund Policy

- UICP does not offer refunds for partial months of service.
- If UICP fails to meet the SLA in any calendar month, the client is
  entitled to a service credit equal to 10% of that month's fee, applied
  to the following month's invoice. Service credits are issued as a
  reduction on the next invoice — not as a cash refund.
- If UICP materially fails to provide the contracted service for a
  continuous period of 30 days or more, the client may terminate the
  agreement and receive a refund for the unused portion of any prepaid
  fees.

---

## Currency

- All prices are listed in **United States dollars (USD)**.
- Clients paying in UGX or EUR will be invoiced at the prevailing
  exchange rate on the invoice date.
- Exchange rate source: Bank of Uganda official rate for UGX; European
  Central Bank rate for EUR.

---

## Disputed Charges

- If a client disputes an invoice, they must notify UICP in writing
  within **15 days** of the invoice date.
- The undisputed portion of the invoice remains due and payable.
- UICP will investigate and respond to disputes within **10 business
  days**.

---

## Taxes

- Prices do not include applicable taxes.
- Clients are responsible for all taxes, duties, and levies imposed by
  their jurisdiction.
- Ugandan clients: 18% VAT will be added to invoices once UICP exceeds
  the VAT registration threshold.
